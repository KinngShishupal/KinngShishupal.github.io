Privacy Policy

Jungle Escape

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

Jungle Escape ("we", "our", or "us") is an endless runner game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or sell any personal information. We do not run our own servers, and the game has no accounts, logins, chat or social features.

The game keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your high score and the total treasure you have collected
  • Settings, such as whether sound is on or off
  • Your ad consent choice, if you were asked for one (see below)

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The only ads in the game are optional rewarded video ads: when you are caught, you may choose to watch ads to get an extra chance. Ads are never shown during gameplay and you can always decline.

To show and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as:

  • The device's Advertising ID
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country)
  • Device model, operating system, app version and language
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched)
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and may be used to show personalized ads where you have allowed it. It is handled under Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Your Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's main menu.

You can also, on any Android device:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device)
  • Delete everything the game has saved by clearing its data or uninstalling it

Children's Privacy

Jungle Escape is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have concerns, please contact us using the details below.

Permissions

The game uses the following permissions:

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads and the consent form. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback when you crash or stumble

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party services in the game are Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (the consent form), both described above. The game does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Data Security and Retention

We do not hold any of your data. What the game saves stays on your device, protected by your device's security, until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit and retained under Google's policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
